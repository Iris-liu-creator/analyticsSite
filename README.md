# Iris Analytics Intelligence

Iris Analytics Intelligence is a professional portfolio and knowledge hub for business analytics, BI reporting, RevOps insights, and practical automation.

The website was built to show how analytics work can move beyond one-off dashboards and become a trusted decision system: clear metrics, reliable reporting foundations, reusable workflows, and business context that helps teams act with more confidence.

## Why This Site Exists

Many business teams have data, reports, and dashboards, but still struggle to answer simple operational questions with confidence. Metrics may be scattered across tools, definitions may be inconsistent, and reporting work can become manual, repetitive, and hard to trust.

This site brings together my work, thinking, and service approach around one central idea: analytics should make business decisions clearer, faster, and more reliable.

## What You Can Find Here

- Analysis articles on BI, reporting quality, metric design, governance, data workflows, and analytics operating models.
- Portfolio-style case studies that show how reporting and dashboard work can support real business decisions.
- Service information for teams that need help clarifying KPIs, improving dashboards, reducing manual reporting, or creating reusable analytics workflows.
- A concise view of my analytics stack, working style, and professional focus.

## The Value

The site is designed for business leaders, analytics teams, hiring managers, and collaborators who want to understand how I approach data work.

It highlights the kind of analytics practice I care about: practical, business-first, technically reliable, and easy for stakeholders to understand and use. The goal is not just to present visuals, but to show how good analytics work can create trust, adoption, and better decisions.

## Technology Note

The website is built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and MDX content. Articles and case studies are written as structured content files, which keeps the site lightweight, maintainable, and easy to expand over time.
