> A sales dashboard should not simply tell leaders what happened. It should help them decide what to do next.

In revenue operations, the real value of reporting is never the visual itself. The true value comes from sharper forecast calls, faster deal intervention, better coaching, cleaner pipeline reviews and more confident decisions. Data-driven organisations are more likely to improve decision-making because they use evidence to guide action, not just opinions or assumptions.

A high-impact sales dashboard should do 3 things:

## Bookings show performance, but segmentation explains it

Closed won and bookings are the scoreboard. But the total number alone does not explain the business.

A strong bookings result may come from one large enterprise deal, strong expansion in existing accounts, or broad new customer growth. Each story requires a different management response.

That is why bookings should always be split by:

<div className="my-8 grid gap-3 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3">

  <div className="rounded-2xl bg-white px-5 py-4 text-sm font-semibold text-slate-950 shadow-sm">Region</div>
  <div className="rounded-2xl bg-white px-5 py-4 text-sm font-semibold text-slate-950 shadow-sm">Company size (Enterprise, mid-market, SMB)</div>
  <div className="rounded-2xl bg-white px-5 py-4 text-sm font-semibold text-slate-950 shadow-sm">Product</div>
  <div className="rounded-2xl bg-white px-5 py-4 text-sm font-semibold text-slate-950 shadow-sm">Sales owner</div>
  <div className="rounded-2xl bg-white px-5 py-4 text-sm font-semibold text-slate-950 shadow-sm">Month, quarter, or year</div>

"We closed $X in Q2 FY26" doesn't tell any story.

The real insight is where growth is coming from, whether it is repeatable, and which part of the business needs support.

## Expose risk at early stage

Pipeline is future potential, not guaranteed revenue. A large pipeline can still be weak if deals are old, poorly qualified or sitting in the wrong forecast category. Dashboard should make risk visible before the miss happens.

## Forecast turns pipeline into judgement

Sales stage shows where the deal sits. Forecast status shows how much confidence the business has.

This creates a better forecast conversation. Instead of asking "how does this deal feel?", leaders can ask:

- What needs to happen to commit the current upsides?
- Can the current strong upside deals close the target gap?
- Which commit deals are actually at risk?
- Which included deals should be excluded?

## Commit versus target

If the target relies heavily on upside, the business is carrying forecast risk. When commit is strong and most of the current-quarter target is already covered, the quarter may be under control. However, that does not automatically mean future-period pipeline coverage is healthy.

Pipeline coverage, target gap and forecast risk must always be assessed within a specific timeframe. A current-quarter view may show confidence, while next quarter may still require more qualified pipeline to support future revenue.
